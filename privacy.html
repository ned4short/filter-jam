Privacy

Last updated October 31, 2026.

No accounts, no personal info

Filter Jam doesn't ask for your name, email or any other personal information, and there's nothing to sign up for.

Your progress

Your level progress and best scores are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data resets them.

Visit statistics

We count visits with Ned Traffic, our own simple visitor counter. It records the page visited, the referring site, your browser type and a random session ID that resets when you close the tab. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they may log your IP address to deliver the pages.
